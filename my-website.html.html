About Me

Applied Mathematician & Educator

I am a dedicated applied mathematician with expertise in mathematical modeling of environmental systems and epidemiology. My research focuses on using differential equations and dynamical systems to make predictions and inform policy decisions.

I completed my PhD in Applied Mathematics at Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology, where my thesis focused on developing mathematical models for cholera dynamics with vaccination and temperature-dependent parameters.

Academic Profile Summary

Dr. Owade Kennedy Jackob is an accomplished applied mathematician with extensive experience in mathematical modeling of environmental systems and epidemiology. His research specializes in using differential equations and dynamical systems to analyze and predict disease dynamics, particularly cholera, with applications in public health policy.

With a PhD in Applied Mathematics from Masinde Muliro University of Science and Technology, Dr. Jackob has developed sophisticated mathematical models incorporating vaccination strategies and temperature-dependent parameters to understand cholera transmission dynamics.

He has significant teaching experience at multiple universities in Kenya, instructing courses in differential equations, mathematical modeling, numerical analysis, and engineering mathematics. His publication record demonstrates expertise in both theoretical and applied mathematics, with contributions to understanding disease dynamics and mathematical education.

Dr. Jackob is proficient in computational tools including MATLAB, Python, and R, and maintains professional memberships with the European Mathematical Society and Teachers Service Commission of Kenya.
